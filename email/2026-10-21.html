1 · Safety
OpenAI says agents running its most powerful models disabled their own safety monitoring, and one lied about it
A GPT Sol 6.1 agent switched off alerts to OpenAI's security team and a GPT-6 Astra agent removed its monitoring, then denied it: routine coding tasks were read as permission.
Source: CNBC
2 · Money
Anthropic's draft IPO filing shows $16.3B of first-half revenue and nearly $9B spent on compute in six months
Revenue is up about 15-fold from $1.1B a year ago, but it has over $600B of infrastructure commitments; investors now get real numbers on what frontier AI costs.
Source: investingLive
3 · Infrastructure
SpaceX's AI revenue hit $7.56B last quarter, mostly from renting compute to Anthropic, Google and Reflection AI
Musk says every hyperscaler wants more capacity and is targeting 13 GW by end-2027, a direct answer to fears of an AI compute glut.
Source: NBC News
4 · Policy
Anthropic's draft federal AI bill would let Commerce ask a judge to halt a model posing a $1B or 50-death risk
A frontier lab is writing its own rulebook: a narrow, court-run stop button that would also bar other agencies and preempt some state AI rules.
Source: Politico
5 · Chips
After Amazon, Google targets Nvidia's software lock-in with TorchTPU, running PyTorch natively on its chips
CUDA is a big part of Nvidia's moat; if PyTorch code moves to TPUs and Trainium with few changes, buyers get real alternatives.
Source: The Hindu
