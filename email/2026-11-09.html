The stories that matter for AI stocks
1 · Infrastructure
Anthropic reportedly locked in $3.4 billion of gas-turbine generator orders (about 1.7 gigawatts) for future AI data centers, taking over existing orders from Texas-based OnePWR Solutions, per the Financial Times
Constellation's earnings call and Anthropic's shopping list are the same story: power is the bottleneck. Neither company had confirmed the deal when Reuters reported it.

2 · Policy
Trump said he doesn't mind AI guardrails and could work with Democrats on safeguards, while warning regulation shouldn't leave the US behind China
The AI trade just had a rough week; a policy that is 'open to guardrails' is a headline the market will read both ways.
Source: The Neuron
3 · Agents
Alibaba's real-world coding-agent test found the top model fully completed only 26.4% of selected tasks, even as the same kinds of models score above 99% on older benchmarks
The robots are great at tests and still mediocre at Mondays. Agent hype vs. agent reality, in one number.
