🤖 AI in brief
The stories that matter for AI stocks
1 · IPO
Anthropic is pushing ahead with a pre-Thanksgiving IPO anyway: $80–100 billion raise at a $1.25–1.75 trillion valuation, below the $2 trillion some backers dreamed of, per investors cited by the FT
The AI rout just gave the biggest IPO of the year a haircut, and they're showing up to the barber anyway. OpenAI also trimmed its private round to $1.25 trillion. A soft print here is a mood ring for every AI stock.
Source: The Decoder
2 · Chips
The U.S. cleared a Chinese firm with an empty Beijing office, and owners tied to a blacklisted private-equity firm, to buy Nvidia H200 chips, per a New York Times investigation
Export controls, but make it honor system. Expect Washington to tighten the screws, which is a headline risk for Nvidia ahead of its Nov 17 report.
Source: The Verge
3 · Talent
Meta's chief strategy officer and former CFO Dave Wehner is leaving to become CFO of Fireworks, a $17.5 billion AI inference startup reportedly in talks to raise at $30–40 billion
When Meta's old money guy picks the picks-and-shovels startup, that's a tell about where he thinks the AI cash lands: running the models, not just building them.
Source: CNBC
